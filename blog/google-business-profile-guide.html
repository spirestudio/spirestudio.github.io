How to get your business on Google Maps. Free, in 20 minutes.

When someone searches "plumber near me" or "cafe in Lichfield", the first thing they usually see is a map with three businesses on it. Getting into that box is free, and it's one of the best things you can do for a local business. It's called a Google Business Profile, and here's how to set one up.

What is a Google Business Profile?

It's your free listing on Google Search and Google Maps. It shows your name, address, phone number, opening hours, photos and reviews. Customers can call you, get directions or visit your website straight from it.

Step 1: Check if you're already listed

Search your business name and town on Google Maps. Google sometimes creates listings automatically. If you're there, look for a link saying "Own this business?" or "Claim this business" and follow it. If not, carry on below.

Step 2: Create your profile

  1. Go to google.com/business and sign in with a Google account. Use a business email if you have one.
  2. Type your business name and pick the category that fits best, like "Barber shop" or "Plumber". You can add more categories later.
  3. Choose whether customers visit you (add your address) or you visit them (add the areas you cover, like Lichfield, Burntwood and Tamworth).
  4. Add your phone number and website.

Step 3: Verify it's really you

Google needs to check you're the owner. Depending on your business, it'll ask for a short video of your premises, a phone call or text, or a postcard to your address. Your profile won't show publicly until this is done, so don't skip it.

Step 4: Fill in everything

Complete profiles rank better. Make sure you add:

  • Opening hours, including bank holidays
  • At least 10 real photos of your shop, team and work
  • A short description of what you do and where
  • Your services or menu with prices

Step 5: Get reviews

Reviews are one of the biggest factors in who appears at the top. After a good job or a happy visit, just ask: "Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review?" Your profile gives you a short link you can text or WhatsApp to customers. Reply to every review, good and bad, politely.

Step 6: Link it to your website

Your profile and your website work best together. The profile gets you seen on the map, and the website convinces people to choose you. Google also trusts a business more when the name, address and phone number match exactly on both.
