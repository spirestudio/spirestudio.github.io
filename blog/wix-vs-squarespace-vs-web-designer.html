Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy or a web designer? Which is best for a small business.

If you're thinking about a website, you've probably seen adverts for Wix, Squarespace and GoDaddy promising a site in minutes. They're genuinely good tools. But they're not the right fit for everyone, and the real cost is often time, not money. Here's an honest comparison.

Website builder prices change often and usually go up after the first year, so always check the renewal price, not just the introductory offer.

Wix

Wix gives you the most freedom: drag anything anywhere. That's great if you have a good eye, but it's also why lots of Wix sites end up looking cluttered, especially on phones. Expect to spend a good few evenings getting it right.

Squarespace

Squarespace templates look very polished out of the box, which makes it popular with salons, cafes and photographers. It's less flexible than Wix, so you're working within the template's style. Strong photos make a big difference.

GoDaddy

GoDaddy's builder is the quickest way to get a basic page live. It asks you a few questions and builds something for you. The downside is that the results look similar to many other businesses, and there's less room to stand out.

Hiring a web designer

A designer builds the site around your business: your photos, your services, your customers. You don't have to learn anything, and a good designer will set it up so customers can call or message you in one tap and Google knows where you are.

The trade-off is the up-front cost. For a local business, expect anything from around £100 to £1,500 depending on who you use. See how much a website costs for a full breakdown.

What about getting found on Google?

All of these can rank on Google. What matters more is what's on the site: your town and services written clearly, fast loading on phones, real photos, and a Google Business Profile with reviews. A builder gives you the tools; it's up to you to use them well. A designer should handle it for you.

So which should you choose?

  • Choose a website builder if you enjoy this kind of thing, have spare time, and want to make changes yourself.
  • Choose a web designer if you'd rather spend your time running the business, and want something that looks professional from day one.
