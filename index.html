Is the demo really free?

Yes. We build your demo website for free. If you don't love it, you don't pay anything and there's no obligation.

Most demos are ready within a few days. Once you're happy, we can usually have it live on your own web address within a day or two.

Nope. We handle everything: design, hosting and setting up your web address. You just tell us what you want.

Of course. On the Plus plan, just message us whenever you want to change prices, photos, hours or anything else.

What's the web address cost?

A .co.uk address is usually around £10 a year, paid directly to the provider so it's always yours.
