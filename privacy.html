Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Spire Studio collects, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short and in plain English. If anything is unclear, just ask us.

Who we are

Spire Studio is a web design business run by Reuben, a sole trader based in Lichfield, Staffordshire. For this website and our services, we are the "controller" of your personal information under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Contact: websitebuilds01@gmail.com

Visiting this website

  • No cookies, no tracking. This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub records technical information such as your IP address in its server logs to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts. We load our typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. Google does not set cookies for this.

If we contact your business and you'd rather we didn't, just reply "no thanks" or "stop" and we will not contact you again. We keep a short do-not-contact list so this sticks.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it with the services we use to run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • Website hosting: GitHub and Netlify
  • Email and messaging: Google (Gmail), Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook)
  • Domain registration, for setting up your web address
  • An accountant or HMRC, if required by law

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where your information leaves the UK, it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK-US data bridge or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go any further: deleted within 12 months.
  • Client records and invoices: kept for 6 years, as required for tax purposes.
  • Your website content: kept while we host your site, and deleted within 3 months after our work together ends, unless you ask us to keep it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop or limit how we use it, including stopping all marketing messages
  • Send your information to you or another business in a usable format

Email websitebuilds01@gmail.com and we'll respond within one month. It's free.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if anything changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
